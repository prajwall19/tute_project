Reporting is easy. Hearing back is the hard part.

Many people already notice problems in their street. What’s missing is a simple way to report one with the exact spot and a photo, and a way to see what happened next. CityFix focuses on both.

How it works

  1. Snap and describe

    Choose the type of problem, add a photo, and share your location or type the landmark.

  2. Send it

    Your report goes into the city feed with the details someone needs to find it.

  3. Follow it

    Watch the progress bar move from submitted to acknowledged, in progress, and resolved.

What’s in CityFix

Quick reports

Six common problem types, a photo, and one-tap location.

Progress tracking

A clear four-step status on every report.

Public city feed

See what others reported and filter open or resolved issues.

Privacy-minded

Photo tips remind people to avoid faces and number plates.
